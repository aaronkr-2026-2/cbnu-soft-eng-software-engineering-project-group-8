# Personas

## Persona 1: Student

### Personal
- **Name:** Ahmed Khan
- **Age:** 21
- **Situation:** University student who wants to improve his academic performance.

### Job
- Attends classes, completes assignments, and prepares for exams.
- Monitors his academic progress.

### Education
- Undergraduate university student.
- Has basic computer skills.

### Relevance
- Wants to understand his expected academic performance.
- Wants to identify areas where he may need improvement.

### User Story
> As a student, I want to enter my academic information and get a performance prediction so that I can understand my academic situation and improve my performance.

## Persona 2: Teacher

### Personal
- **Name:** Sarah Lee
- **Age:** 35
- **Situation:** University teacher who monitors student progress.

### Job
- Teaches university students.
- Reviews student academic performance and provides support.

### Education
- University graduate with experience using computer-based systems.
- Has basic knowledge of data and academic records.

### Relevance
- Wants to identify students who may need additional academic support.
- Uses performance information to better understand student progress.

### User Story
> As a teacher, I want to view student performance predictions so that I can identify students who may need additional academic support.
